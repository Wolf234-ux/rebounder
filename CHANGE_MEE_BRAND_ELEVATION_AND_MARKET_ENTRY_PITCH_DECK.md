# SECTION 2: DOMESTIC INDIAN MARKET ENTRY STRATEGY

### 1. The Real Indian Problem: "The Senior Walking Deficit"
Doctors in India universally tell seniors: *"Roj 45 minute chala"* (Walk 45 minutes every day). But in reality, millions of Indian seniors cannot walk outdoors:
1. **Broken Infrastructure:** Uneven pavers, open storm drains, reckless two-wheelers, stray animals, and zero ramps.
2. **Brutal Seasons:** 4 months of heavy monsoon downpours + 4 months of 42°C summer heat + winter smog traps elders indoors for 8+ months every year.
3. **Severe Osteoarthritis:** India has over 180 million arthritis sufferers. Concrete road walking delivers 3.2x body weight shock directly into thinning knee cartilage.

### 2. The Core Buyer Persona: "The Guilt-Driven Caregiver" (Ages 38–58)
* **Who Actually Buys:** Not the 72-year-old grandmother. It is her **45-year-old son or daughter** (IT VP in Bengaluru, doctor in Pune, corporate director in Mumbai, or NRI living abroad).
* **The Emotional Driver:** Filial duty and guilt. They watch their parents lose independence, sit in front of the TV all day, gain weight, and struggle to stand from the sofa.
* **The Value Proposition:**  
  *"Give your parents 45 minutes of doctor-prescribed walking benefits in 10 minutes at home—with zero joint pain, zero fall risk, in living room comfort."*
* **The Pricing Logic:**  
  ₹43,000 amortizes across a 4-person joint family to just **₹29 per day**. Compared to ₹72,000 spent annually on physio visits and painkillers, Change Mee pays for itself in under 3 months.
