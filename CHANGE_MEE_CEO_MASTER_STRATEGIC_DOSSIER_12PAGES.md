Section 1: Biomechanical & Clinical Foundations
A. Deceleration Impulse Physics & Shock Elimination: Landing on an elastic surface is governed by the impulse-momentum
theorem: J = ∫ F dt = ∆p. Metal springs possess high mechanical stiffness and reach their elongation limit abruptly within ∆t ≈ 0.05 s,
generating a sharp peak force spike (F_peak = ∆p / ∆t) that transfers directly into ankle ligaments, knee cartilage, and lumbar discs.
Change Mee's 36-cord elastic bungee suspension extends deceleration time (∆t ≈ 0.20 s) by 300% to 400%, absorbing kinetic
energy progressively and reducing peak joint shock by up to 85%.
B. Lymphatic Dynamics & Vertical G-Force Acceleration: The human lymphatic system contains 2 to 3 times more fluid than
blood, yet lacks an intrinsic muscular pump. It depends on muscle contractions and vertical gravitational acceleration shifts.
Rebounding alternates forces between 0G at the crest and 2G at bottom deceleration. This rhythmic pressure gradient opens and
closes millions of microscopic one-way semilunar valves in lymphatic vessels, accelerating fluid return toward the thoracic duct,
relieving peripheral edema, and supporting immune circulation.
